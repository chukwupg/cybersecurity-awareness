# 📱 Mobile Safety

**Who this is for:** Smartphone users (personal & work devices), BYOD users, parents, and IT learners.  
This guide covers device setup, app hygiene, network risks, and what to do after a compromise.

---

## Overview
Mobile devices hold messages, contacts, banking apps, photos, and work data. 
Attacks include malware delivered via apps or links, SIM swapping, phishing SMS (smishing), malicious Wi-Fi, and targeted social engineering. 

- Securing your phone requires both technical controls and daily habits.

---

## Common mobile threats
- **Malicious apps and sideloading:** Apps outside official stores may contain malware.  
- **Smishing (SMS phishing):** Fraudulent SMS that prompt you to click links or call numbers.  
- **SIM swap attacks:** Attackers trick the phone carrier to transfer your number and bypass SMS 2FA.  
- **Evil twin Wi-Fi & man-in-the-middle:** Fake Wi-Fi hotspots that intercept data.  
- **Unpatched OS vulnerabilities:** Missing updates expose you to remote exploits.  
- **Physical theft / device loss:** Direct access to the device and data if not protected

---

## Secure device setup (first steps)
1. **Use a strong device passcode** (not 4-digit if possible) and enable biometric unlock if available.  
2. **Enable device encryption** — modern phones typically encrypt storage by default when a passcode is set.  
3. **Turn on automatic OS updates** and update apps regularly.  
4. **Enable Find My Device / Find My iPhone** and set up remote wipe.  
5. **Set up backup** (encrypted) so you can restore data if needed.

---

## App hygiene & permissions
- **Install apps only from official stores** (Google Play, Apple App Store).  
- **Avoid sideloading** APKs or third-party stores unless you fully trust the source.  
- **Review app permissions** before and after installing: location, camera, microphone, contacts, SMS — revoke unnecessary ones.  
- **Limit background access** where possible (battery & privacy settings).  
- **Check app reviews, developer name, and download counts** — fake apps often have low engagement or suspicious developer details.

---

## Network & connectivity safety
- **Avoid public Wi-Fi for sensitive tasks.** If needed, use a **trusted VPN**.  
- **Turn off auto-join for Wi-Fi networks** and Bluetooth when not in use.  
- **Forget networks you no longer use.**  
- **Use cellular data for critical operations** when on untrusted networks.  
- **Disable Wi-Fi and Bluetooth when sleeping or in public** to reduce attack surface.

---

## SMS, calls, and SIM security
- **Be skeptical of messages asking to confirm codes or login info.** Legit services will not ask for your password or any other sensitive information by SMS.  
- **Set up a PIN with your mobile carrier** to protect against SIM swap social engineering.  
- **Use app-based authenticators or hardware keys** for 2FA when possible, rather than SMS-based codes.

---

## Secure messaging & privacy
- Prefer **end-to-end encrypted** messaging apps (e.g., Signal, iMessage for Apple users).  
- **Turn off message previews** on the lock screen to avoid exposing sensitive content.  
- **Understand backup encryption** — some apps encrypt local messages but cloud backups may be unencrypted.

---

## Backups & recovery
- Use **encrypted backups** (local or cloud) and test restore procedures.  
- Keep a copy of important account recovery codes (e.g., 2FA backup codes) in a secure place like a password manager.

---

## Physical device security
- **Use a screen lock** with biometric + PIN/strong passcode fallback.  
- **Avoid leaving your device unattended** in public places.  
- **Cover cameras** or use camera-blocking/privacy covers if you are concerned about unwanted access.

---

## What to do if your phone is lost or stolen
1. Use Find My Device (Android) / Find My iPhone (Apple) to locate or remotely lock the device.  
2. If you can’t recover it, **perform a remote wipe.**  
3. Change passwords for accounts accessible via the device (email, banking, social).  
4. Contact your carrier to report theft and consider suspending the SIM.  
5. Monitor bank accounts and enable fraud alerts.

---

## Signs your phone may be compromised
- Battery drains unusually fast.  
- Unexplained data usage spikes.  
- Unecessary/unwanted Pop-ups
- Apps you don’t remember installing.  
- Unusual behavior like sending messages you didn’t write.  
- Unknown device administrator apps (Android).

---

## Incident response (if you suspect compromise)
1. **Isolate the device:** turn off Wi-Fi/data, or power off.  
2. **Scan** using reputable mobile security apps.  
3. **Backup** important files (if possible) and perform a factory reset.  
4. **Restore from a known-good backup.**  
5. **Change passwords** from a trusted device and review account sessions.  
6. **Revoke app permissions** and reinstall apps only from official stores.

---

## Parental & BYOD (Bring Your Own Device) tips
- Use parental controls and family sharing settings to limit app installs and purchases.  
- For BYOD, **segregate work data** using company profiles or secure containers and follow employer security policies.  
- Educate BYOD workers on common mobile threats (with emphasis on smishing and suspicious links)
- Educate family members on common mobile threats.

---

## Recommended apps & features (examples)
- **Find My Device / Find My iPhone** — location & remote wipe.  
- **Signal, iMessage** — private messaging with end-to-end encryption.  
- **Password manager** (Passwords (Apple), Bitwarden, 1Password) — generate and store strong passwords with 2FA codes.  
- **Official app stores** — always install from trusted app stores (Google Play store or Apple Appstore)
- **Carrier PIN** — ask your mobile provider to enable.

---

## Quick checklist
- [ ] Device is protected by a strong passcode and biometric lock  
- [ ] OS and apps auto-update enabled  
- [ ] Only official app stores are used  
- [ ] Backups are enabled and tested (ensure they are encrypted)  
- [ ] Find My Device/iPhone is enabled and remote wipe is possible  
- [ ] Carrier/account PIN is set to prevent SIM swap
