# 📲 Social Media Safety

**Who this is for:** Social media users, content creators, professionals, and anyone managing an online presence.  
This guide explains how to protect privacy, avoid scams, and manage reputation on social platforms.

---

## Overview
Social media is powerful for connection and visibility, but it also exposes personal information, opens avenues for scams and impersonation, and can be used for social engineering. 
Protecting accounts and managing the content you share are key to long-term safety.

---

## Common risks
- **Oversharing:** Personal details (birthdays, addresses, travel plans) used for identity theft or targeted attacks.  
- **Impersonation / fake profiles:** Scammers create accounts that mimic real people or brands.  
- **Malicious links & scams:** Links in DMs or posts that lead to phishing or malware.  
- **Doxxing / unwanted exposure:** Personal info published publicly to harass or harm.  
- **Social engineering:** Attackers use personal info to build trust and exploit you.

---

## Account hardening
1. **Use unique, strong passwords** for each social account (see [`general/passwords.md`](/general/passwords.md)).  
2. **Enable 2-Factor Authentication:** prefer app-based authenticators or hardware keys.  
3. **Save recovery & backup codes** in a password manager or secure vault.  
4. **Review active sessions** and log out remote devices you don’t recognize.  
   - Typical path: *Settings → Security → Where you’re logged in / Active sessions*  
5. **Remove old linked apps** and revoke access for apps you no longer use.

---

## Privacy settings & audience control
- **Set account visibility** (private for personal accounts where appropriate).  
- **Limit post audience** to friends/contacts for non-public content.  
- **Disable location sharing** on posts unless necessary.  
- **Turn off automatic sharing** to other platforms unless intentional.  
- **Review tagged content** and enable manual approval of tags if available.

---

## Content hygiene: think before you post
- Avoid posting **sensitive details**: phone numbers, home address, travel dates, full date-of-birth, government IDs, or copies of documents.  
- Avoid posting images containing **metadata (EXIF)** that reveals location — strip EXIF before uploading if needed.  
- Be cautious with “check-in” posts that reveal current location in real time.  
- Use **generic language** if you want to share about travel or events without exact details.

---

## Handling friend/follower requests & messages
- **Verify unknown requests** by checking mutual connections or reaching out through another channel.  
- **Be wary of urgent requests** in DMs requesting money or help; confirm identity with voice/video call or other out-of-band checks.  
- **Don’t click shortened links** from unknown senders; preview or expand them first.

---

## Detecting impersonation & scams
- Impersonation signs: slight spelling changes in usernames, few posts, low followers but aggressive DMs.  
- Scams often use **urgency** (“send money now”) or **reward** promises (fake giveaways).  
- If you suspect impersonation, report the account to the platform and warn your followers.

---

## Reputation & professional use
- **Separate personal and professional accounts** where possible. Keep sensitive opinions off public professional profiles.  
- **Audit your history** periodically and remove old posts that may harm your reputation.  
- **Set up Google Alerts** for your name or brand to be notified about mentions (or use platform notifications).

---

## Photo & metadata hygiene
- **Strip EXIF metadata** from photos before posting if you’re concerned about revealing location.  
- Many phones and apps offer an option to remove location data at upload time, enable it if not enabled by default.

---

## What to do if your account is compromised
1. **Attempt to log in and change the password immediately** from a trusted device.  
2. **Use account recovery** flows and submit ID if platform requires it.  
3. **Revoke sessions & connected apps** once you regain access.  
4. **Notify followers** if messages were sent from your account to avoid further spread.  
5. **Report to the platform** and follow their compromise/recovery procedures.  
6. **Check linked accounts** (email, other socials) and change those passwords too.

---

## Managing automation & third-party apps
- Only grant permissions to apps you trust and review permissions before approval.  
- Revoke tokens for unused or suspicious apps: *Settings → Apps & Websites / Connected apps*.  
- Be careful with scheduling tools that require broad permissions.

---

## Children, teens & guardians
- Teach kids not to share personal details and to set accounts to private.  
- Use parental controls and family sharing.
- Educate them about stranger requests and risky content.  
- Encourage them to come to you if they see something suspicious.

---

## Quick checklist
- [ ] Unique strong password + 2FA enabled for all social accounts  
- [ ] Privacy settings reviewed and minimized where appropriate  
- [ ] Location sharing disabled by default  
- [ ] Third-party apps reviewed and untrusted apps removed  
- [ ] Photo metadata removed before posting sensitive images  
- [ ] Have a plan for account recovery and store backup codes securely
