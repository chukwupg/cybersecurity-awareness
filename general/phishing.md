# 🎣 Phishing Awareness

Phishing is one of the most common cyberattacks. Attackers trick you into revealing sensitive information, such as passwords or credit card numbers, by pretending to be someone you trust.  

---

## 📧 Common Types of Phishing
- **Email Phishing** – Fake emails from “banks” or “services” asking you to click a link.  
- **Spear Phishing** – Targeted attacks against individuals or organizations.  
- **Smishing** – Fake SMS messages.  
- **Vishing** – Voice calls pretending to be from support or banks.  

---

## ⚠️ How to Spot a Phishing Attempt
- Urgent or threatening language (“Your account will be locked!”).  
- Suspicious links (hover to preview before clicking).  
- Unexpected attachments.  
- Sender address doesn’t match the real organization.  

---

## ✅ How to Protect Yourself
- Double-check URLs before clicking.  
- Never share personal info via email or SMS.  
- Report suspicious emails to your IT/security team.  
- Use security tools (email filters, anti-phishing browser extensions).  

---

## 🚩 Quick Checklist
- [ ] Verify links before clicking  
- [ ] Don’t download unexpected attachments  
- [ ] Report phishing attempts immediately
